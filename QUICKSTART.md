# 🚀 Quick Start Guide - FLEXPERT

## Getting Started in 3 Steps

### 1️⃣ Open the Application
Simply open `index.html` in any modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)

### 2️⃣ Answer the Questions
You'll be guided through 7 questions:
- **Workout Goal** - What you want to achieve
- **Body Type** - Your current physique
- **Training Frequency** - How many days you can commit
- **Physical Limitations** - Any injuries or health concerns
- **Workout Environment** - Home or Gym
- **Intensity Level** - How hard you want to push
- **Duration** - Time available per session

### 3️⃣ Get Your Plan
Click "Get My Plan" and receive:
- ✅ **Strategy** - Your personalized approach
- ✅ **Exercises** - Specific movements to perform
- ✅ **Schedule** - Training frequency recommendation
- ✅ **Equipment** - What you'll need
- ✅ **Intensity** - Target effort level
- ✅ **Expert Advice** - Professional tips and safety notes
